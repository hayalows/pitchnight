PRINTING & ASSEMBLY +

For each handheld sign, print at actual size on A4 landscape, then mount it on 3–5 mm foam board or lightweight PVC. Cut along the outside white edge and attach a flat wooden or sturdy card handle behind the lower centre. Have the print shop check colour and dimensions before cutting the whole set.

For a larger-than-A4 prop, give the printer the SVG rather than stretching a social PNG. The type is editable SVG text and may need converting to outlines in the print shop’s software to preserve the font.
