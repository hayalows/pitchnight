002 / How a night goes

One night.
Four parts.

This is how Pitch Night 001 ran. The props are the real ones from the night.

01/ 04
  1. 01

    Hellos & welcome.

    Walk in, find a seat and meet the room. Nobody needs to know anyone yet. That is the point.

    {{PROP_03}}
  2. 02

    Games & warm-up.

    A few quick games loosen everyone up. Strangers start laughing at the same things.

    {{PROP_05}}
  3. 03

    Pitch your friend.

    Friends take the mic and make the case for someone they love. The room listens, cheers and asks the hard questions.

    {{PROP_01}}
  4. 04

    The good part.

    The pitches end and the real conversations begin. Some of the best moments happen after the mic goes down.
