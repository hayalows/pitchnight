OffScript brings people together through events that make space for conversation, creativity and real connections.

We began with Pitch Night: Make Your Case, an evening in Accra where people came to speak up for their friends, share a laugh and meet someone new. We’re only getting started.

    Hellos & welcome.

    Games & warm-up.

    Pitch your friend.

    Friends take the mic and make the case for someone they love. The room listens, cheers and asks the hard questions.
