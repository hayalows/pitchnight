ABOUT THE EMAIL LIST

WHAT WE KEEP.

When you choose to join the list, we save your email address, when you agreed to receive updates and whether you've confirmed your email. We use that information to send updates about OffScript events.

We use Cloudflare to store subscription information and Resend to deliver emails. We don't sell your email address. You can unsubscribe from the link in any welcome or event-update email.

If you'd like us to delete your subscription record or have a question, email offscript.beyondtheusual@gmail.com.

We also retain a limited, hashed request identifier to help prevent automated signup abuse. This list is separate from any information you might share on an event's Connection Board.
