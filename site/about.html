01 / WHY WE'RE HERE

GOOD THINGS HAPPEN IN A ROOM.

It's easier to meet someone when there's a reason to start talking. That's the kind of experience we want to make.

OffScript is a place for people to share ideas, show up for one another and enjoy the unexpected moments that happen along the way.

We're building this through events, one edition at a time.
