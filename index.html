HI FRIEND! 01 / THANK YOU

First things first, thank you for showing up for OffScript: Pitch Night, Edition 001.

Whether you came ready to pitch, cheer on a friend, meet new people or simply enjoy the evening, we're grateful you spent part of your Friday with us. Your presence, energy and participation made our first edition memorable.

To the volunteers, organisers, host and everyone who helped behind the scenes, thank you for bringing the idea to life.

We also owe you an apology for starting later than planned and finishing late. We appreciate your patience. We've taken note, and we'll do better next time.

The event photos will be shared here when they're ready. We'll also add our Connection Board feedback form so you can tell us what you enjoyed and what we can improve for Edition 002.

If you couldn't make it this time, we'd be glad to have you at a future gathering.

Thank you again for believing in this idea and helping us bring it to life. Edition 001 was the beginning.

Beyond the usual. Into connection.

With gratitude,
The OffScript Team

OFF SCRIPT / MEET THE HOST
THE MAN WITH THE MIC

EDITION 001 HOST

KHEMIKAL.

DANIEL SELASE

Daniel Selase, popularly known as Khemikal, is a Ghanaian stand-up comedian with a passion for comedy and entertainment. He uses humour, storytelling and effective communication to entertain, inspire and connect with people.

His strengths include creative thinking, observation and audience engagement. He communicates ideas in ways that feel relatable and memorable.

He joined Toastmasters to develop his public speaking and leadership skills, strengthen his communication and grow into a more confident and impactful voice, on and off the stage.
